# Race support

Natural-part harvesting uses the anatomy each race provides. Compatible humanoid races can use supported natural-part surgeries without a separate entry on this page. Animal parts can only replace matching parts from the same species.

Tail removal and installation require a real anatomical tail. A tail that only changes a pawn's appearance cannot be removed.

## Race-specific tail support

Dedicated tail items or surgery recipes are included for:

- Big and Small race bodies
- Camerline Kobolds and Venerek Kobolds
- Kurin and Kimiri
- Revia and Moyo
- Dragonian Prime, including its unofficial version
- Silkiera, Ratkin, Kiiro and Rabbie
- Glassy, Mofy, Heruan and Nyaron
- Orassan and Neko
- Tozol
- Yanzihko race packs, including Cheetah, Jaguar, Leo, Panther, Tigris, Fressalfar, Kisalfar, Lombax and Vulpoid
- Logann and Ferrex
- Frosty
- Xenomorph Type
- Races that provide a natural insect tail

Some race packs share the same tail type or use the same tail item. Tail compatibility depends on the race mod defining a natural tail body part.

For general recovery chances, surgery requirements and troubleshooting, see the [FAQ](FAQ.md).
